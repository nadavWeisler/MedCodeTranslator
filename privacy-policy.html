Privacy Policy

Med Code Translator — Last updated: May 2026

Summary

Med Code Translator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data is stored on your device only.

What data we collect

We collect no data. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection.

How data is stored

Medical code databases are stored locally on your device using SQLite. No data is sent to any server.

Third-party services

The app does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notifications).

Children's privacy

The app is a medical reference tool intended for healthcare professionals and students. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

Any future changes will be posted here. We will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page.
